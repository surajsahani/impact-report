STUDENT TECH

🏫 Samadhan — Campus Issue Tracker App!

Solving Campus Problems Instantly

Built by students, for the campus 🔧

Samadhan is a mobile-first app designed by our learners to manage and resolve everyday campus issues. If a bulb is fused, water is leaking, or Wi-Fi is down, students log it instantly to get it resolved!

⚡
Quick Reporting: Log water leaks, broken bulbs, or Wi-Fi bugs in 10 seconds.
🤝
Student-led Action: Student councils claim tickets and fix issues collectively.
📊
Live Tracking: Real-time status cards showing what is pending, active, or resolved.

🤖 AI Learning Labs — Ella & Zoe!

AI-Powered Personalized English Learning

Talk to Zoe & find your English level 💬

Our AI Labs build intelligent tools to guide students individually. Ella is an interactive English learning assistant powered by **Zoe**, our custom conversational learning agent. Students practice speaking, learn vocabulary, and get real-time feedback.

💬
Interactive Dialogues: Conversational language learning mimicking 1-on-1 tutoring.
📝
